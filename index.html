Our Story

Founded in 1999 by Steve Smart and Scott McCartney

1st Choice Home Improvements started with a small office built onto the back of a house and two founders who wanted to give homeowners an honest, dependable alternative for remodeling. More than 25 years later, the principles are the same: quality craftsmanship, honest recommendations and customer satisfaction.
